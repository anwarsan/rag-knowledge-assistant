# Database Recovery Runbook
Synthetic training document. Version 1.0.

## Before recovery
A database specialist must confirm the failing database, recent backup timestamp, replication state, and expected data-loss window. Obtain approval from the incident commander before a recovery operation. Capture diagnostic observations in the incident record.

## Recovery boundary
This illustrative runbook does not specify database-specific restoration commands, credentials, backup locations, recovery time objectives, or recovery point objectives. Consult the service-specific approved recovery plan. Do not infer missing commands from this document.
