# Incident Response SOP
Synthetic training document. Version 2.0. Approved for this illustrative corpus only.

## Critical incident escalation
For a critical service incident that remains unresolved after 15 minutes, the support lead must page the on-call incident commander and the service owner. Open a dedicated incident bridge and record the incident ID, customer impact, actions attempted, and current blockers. The incident commander coordinates responders and assigns a technical lead. Do not wait for the next scheduled status update before escalating.

## Escalation acknowledgement
If the incident commander does not acknowledge within 5 minutes of being paged, page the backup incident commander. Keep a timestamped escalation log. Follow the Communications SOP for stakeholder updates while the technical response continues.

## Resolution and review
The incident commander confirms service recovery using monitoring and verification from the service owner. The support lead records the recovery time, publishes a final status update, and opens a post-incident review task within two business days.
