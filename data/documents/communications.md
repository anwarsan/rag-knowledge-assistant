# Communications SOP
Synthetic training document. Version 1.0.

## Stakeholder updates
During a critical incident, the communications lead posts a stakeholder update every 20 minutes until recovery is confirmed. Include the incident ID, observed impact, known facts, mitigation progress, and time of the next update. State explicitly when a recovery estimate is unknown. Never invent an estimated resolution time.

## Customer information
Use the approved status page for customer-facing updates. Exclude customer personal data, access tokens, internal IP addresses, and confidential investigation details. The incident commander approves the final recovery notice.
